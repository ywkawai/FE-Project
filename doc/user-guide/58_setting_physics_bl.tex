\section{Planetary Boundary Layer Scheme} \label{sec:basic_usel_pbl}
%------------------------------------------------------
The planetary boundary layer (PBL) scheme is parameterized vertical mixing by the PBL turbulence
It is used for Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes equations (RANS) simulations.
The PBL scheme is specified in configured by two namelists.
First, the PBL component is activated by \nmitem{ATMOS_PHY_BL_DO} in \namelist{PARAM_ATMOS}.
Then, the PBL scheme and its calling interval are specified in \namelist{PARAM_ATMOS_PHY_RD}.

\editboxtwo{
 \verb|&PARAM_ATMOS | & \\
 \verb|ATMOS_PHY_BL_DO = .true.,| & ; Enable planetary boundary layer parameterization \\
 \verb|/ | & \\
}
\editboxtwo{
 \verb|&PARAM_ATMOS_PHY_BL | & \\
 \verb|BL_TYPE      = "MYNN_LEVEL2",| & ; Choose from PBL schemes in Table \ref{tab:bl_scheme_list} \\
 \verb|TIME_DT      = 600.D0,|      & ; Time interval of PBL process \\
 \verb|TIME_DT_UNIT = "SEC",|      & ; Unit for \verb|TIME_DT| \\
 \verb|/ | & \\
}

The available choices of \nmitem{BL_TYPE} are listed in Table \ref{tab:bl_scheme_list}.
