\section{Cumulus parameterization}

Cumulus parameterization represents the effects of unresolved moist convection on the resolved atmospheric state.
The cumulus parameterization component is activated by (\verb|ATMOS_PHY_CP_DO|) in [\verb|PARAM_ATMOS|].
The cumulus parameterization scheme and its calling interval are specified in [\verb|PARAM_ATMOS_PHY_CP|].

\editboxtwo{
 \verb|&PARAM_ATMOS | & \\
 \verb|ATMOS_PHY_CP_DO = .true.,| & ; Enable cumulus parameterization \\
 \verb|/ | & \\
}
\editboxtwo{
 \verb|&PARAM_ATMOS_PHY_CP | & \\
 \verb|CP_TYPE      = "MCA",| & ; Choose from cumulus parameterization schemes in Table \ref{tab:cp_scheme_list} \\
 \verb|TIME_DT      = 60.D0,| & ; Time interval of cumulus parameterization process \\
 \verb|TIME_DT_UNIT = "SEC",| & ; Unit for \verb|TIME_DT| \\
 \verb|/ | & \\
}

The available choices of (\verb|CP_TYPE|) are listed in Table \ref{tab:cp_scheme_list}.
(\verb|TIME_DT|) and (\verb|TIME_DT_UNIT|) specify the time interval of the cumulus parameterization process.

\subsection{Moist convective adjustment scheme}
%%%
The moist convective adjustment (MCA) scheme is a simple cumulus parameterization based on the concept of \cite{manabe1965}.
The scheme diagnoses saturated convective instability in nearly saturated atmospheric columns and adjusts unstable layers toward a moist-neutral thermodynamic state.

Saturated convective instability is diagnosed using the vertical distribution of saturated moist static energy (MSE).
When a nearly saturated layer has saturated MSE decreasing with height, a contiguous unstable region is identified and subject to the adjustment.
The adjusted thermodynamic profile is determined so as to satisfy the available-water constraint and conservation of column-integrated MSE.
If a consistent adjusted state cannot be obtained within the initially diagnosed unstable region, the adjustment region is extended vertically and the calculation is repeated.

Water vapor removed by the adjustment is diagnosed as precipitation and is immediately removed from the atmospheric column; no condensate is retained by the MCA scheme.
After each adjustment, the atmospheric state is updated and saturated convective instability is diagnosed again over the entire column.
This diagnosis--adjustment cycle is repeated until no saturated convective instability remains.

The MCA scheme is applied at the interval specified by \nmitem{TIME_DT} and \nmitem{TIME_DT_UNIT} in the cumulus parameterization process.
The precipitation generated during the successive adjustments is accumulated within each call of the scheme and contributes to the surface precipitation flux.
